Meet the Founder

Sayantani
MB.

POSH begins with a simple belief: the future of fashion should feel more considered, not less beautiful. The house brings together technology, storytelling and conscious material choices to create a luxury label for a generation that expects both.

The ambition is deliberately bigger than clothing: build a house with a distinct point of view, an unmistakable digital presence and a product philosophy that can grow from limited editions into a global luxury label.
